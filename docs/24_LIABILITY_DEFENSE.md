# 24 Liability / Incident Defense by Design

Not legal advice. Final commercial terms require jurisdiction-appropriate professional review.

## Engineering controls
1. deterministic Safety Gate before AI tools
2. least privilege and allowlisted worker actions
3. snapshots before AI modification
4. test gates before release
5. release-risk checklist
6. explicit approval for production/destructive/store/billing actions
7. append-oriented audit trail
8. backups and rollback
9. data minimization / customer-owned cloud where selected
10. secret isolation
11. remote revocation / emergency stop in the remote phase
12. region/store rules rechecked at release time

## Product controls
- show when output is generated/assisted by AI where relevant
- do not promise bug-free, legally guaranteed, or review-guaranteed output
- distinguish draft/preview from production
- give users a clear review/approval screen
- preserve evidence of tests, approvals, versions, and releases
- define support, security-reporting, incident, suspension, and appeal flows before public launch

## Commercial launch gate
Before accepting paying public users: legal review of Terms, Privacy Policy, limitation-of-liability wording, consumer/subscription rules, data processing, store terms, and target-market obligations; plus cyber/IT liability insurance review.
